BUGATTI

The Bugatti W16 Mistral “Blanc Éternel” is a one-of-one bespoke version of the brand’s final W16-powered roadster. Finished in an elegant white “Eternal White” theme, it features porcelain details created in collaboration with KPM Berlin and combines sculptural craftsmanship with Bugatti’s quad-turbo W16 engine producing around 1,578 horsepower. It represents a highly exclusive, art-focused interpretation of the Mistral and one of the final expressions of Bugatti’s legendary W16 era.

  • “W” layout: Two V-shaped engines joined together at a shared crankshaft
  • 16 cylinders total: Four banks of 4 cylinders
  • Quad-turbocharged: Uses 4 turbochargers to force more air into the engine
  • Evolution: First used in the Bugatti Veyron, perfected in the Chiron, and finalized in the Mistral
  • This layout allowed massive power while still fitting inside a (relatively) road-legal car chassis
